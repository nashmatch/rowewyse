How to Use This Map

1

Choose a DPA Program

Use the DPA Program menu on the right panel to select a program. Each button switches the map to show that program's eligible areas — colored areas are eligible, grey areas are not.

2

Search Your Address

Type a Shelby County address into the Check an Address box and press the arrow. The map will drop a pin and instantly show you which programs that address qualifies for — even across multiple programs at once.

3

Click Any Area

Tap or click any shaded area on the map to see details about that Census Tract or ZIP Code and whether it qualifies for the active program.

4

Check Your Eligibility

Click Check Eligibility in the top right to fill out a quick form. We'll rank which programs are the best fit for you based on your credit score, income, and debt — and notify your realtor automatically.
